What We Design

Fully bespoke, by appointment.

Every AFUVAI project is custom-designed. Below is where we focus — and what it starts at.

Discipline I

Weddings

From intimate Las Vegas elopements to full-scale celebrations, we design wedding florals that hold the eye and the memory — bridal bouquets, ceremony arches and installations, aisle and altar florals, reception centerpieces, and statement moments.

  • Bridal & bridal party bouquets
  • Ceremony arches, chuppahs & floral installations
  • Reception centerpieces & tablescapes
  • On-site styling, delivery & teardown
Starting at $3,500

Discipline II

Private & Corporate Events

Galas, milestone birthdays, anniversaries, dinners, and corporate functions. We create centerpieces and large-scale floral art that transform the room and reflect the occasion — and the brand, when there is one.

  • Statement centerpieces & tablescapes
  • Large-scale installations & backdrops
  • Corporate & brand-aligned palettes
  • Full delivery & on-site installation
Starting at $1,500

Discipline III

Floral-Making Classes & Private Events

Learn to build something beautiful with your own hands. Join an intimate public workshop, or host your own private floral-making event — a bridal shower, team gathering, or celebration. We bring the blooms, tools, and step-by-step guidance.

  • Small-group public workshops
  • Private "host your own" floral events
  • All premium blooms, tools & guidance included
  • In-studio or at your venue
