Meet the Founder

AFUVAI was founded by Ami Nelson, a Las Vegas floral designer with a background in luxury hospitality and a love for design that lingers in memory long after the event ends. After years immersed in the city's high-end hospitality world, Ami built AFUVAI to bring that same standard of detail, discretion, and showmanship to floral design.

Every AFUVAI arrangement is created by hand, to order, for one occasion and one client. No two are alike — because no two moments are.
