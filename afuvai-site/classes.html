Hands-On

Make something beautiful.

Join an intimate floral-making class in our studio, or host your own private floral event. We bring the blooms, the tools, and the guidance — you leave with an arrangement and a memory.

What to Expect

An evening in the studio.

Each class is small, relaxed, and fully guided. We start with a glass of something lovely, walk through technique and design, then build a seasonal arrangement you'll take home. Every seat includes premium blooms, tools, and a step-by-step from our designers.

  • All premium blooms & tools included
  • Beginner-friendly, fully guided
  • Take home everything you make
  • Perfect for date nights, friends & celebrations
