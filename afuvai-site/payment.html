How It Works

Custom work, simple payment.

Because every custom AFUVAI project is bespoke, we don't sell fixed-price products here. After your consultation you'll receive a proposal — then use the options below. (Ordering a bouquet or class seat? Those are paid at checkout in the shop and classes pages.)

Request a Link
Setup note (remove before launch): Connect each button to a Stripe Payment Link or hosted invoice. Recommended setup — create the AFUVAI business entity, connect Stripe, then generate (1) an open-amount deposit link, (2) per-client invoices for balances, and (3) a custom-amount link. Stripe fees are 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction with no monthly cost. The same Stripe account powers the shop (via Snipcart) and class-seat links.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. AFUVAI does not store your card details. By submitting payment you agree to the terms outlined in your proposal.
