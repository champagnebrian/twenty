Questions

Good to know.

The questions we're asked most. Don't see yours? Reach out →

Where is AFUVAI located and what areas do you serve?

AFUVAI is a by-appointment luxury floral studio based in Las Vegas, Nevada. We don't operate a walk-in retail store. We design and deliver across Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and venues throughout The Strip.

Do you have a minimum order or starting price?

Wedding floral design starts at $3,500 and private and corporate events start at $1,500. Floral-making class seats start at $125, and signature bouquets in our online shop start around $155. Every custom project is quoted based on scope, flowers, and installation.

Can I order a bouquet online for delivery?

Yes. Our online shop offers a curated, rotating collection of hand-tied signature bouquets for delivery across the Las Vegas valley. Order by 1pm for next-day delivery. A recurring floral subscription service is coming soon.

How do floral classes and private floral events work?

We host small-group public workshops you can reserve a seat for online, and private "host your own" floral events for groups of six or more — in our studio or at your venue. Every class includes premium blooms, tools, and step-by-step guidance, and you take home what you make.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, we recommend booking 3 to 9 months ahead to secure your date and source premium blooms. Classes and bouquet orders work on much shorter timelines.

What makes AFUVAI different from other Las Vegas florists?

We're fully bespoke and built on a background in luxury hospitality. Beyond weddings and events, we offer hands-on floral-making classes and private floral experiences, and our online shop delivers signature bouquets across the valley.

How does payment work?

Online bouquet orders and class seats are paid securely at checkout. For custom work, after your consultation you receive a proposal; a deposit (typically 50%) secures your date, with the balance due before your event. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Can you work with my wedding planner or venue?

Absolutely. We regularly coordinate with planners, venues, and other vendors to deliver and install seamlessly. Share your planner and venue details in your inquiry and we'll handle the logistics.

What flowers do you use?

We source premium and rare blooms to order, selected for each project's palette, season, and design. Because we buy per event rather than stocking a cooler, we can pursue specific varieties and color stories you won't find in standard arrangements.
