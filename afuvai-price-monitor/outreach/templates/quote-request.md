# Quote Request Template — Phase 0 Baseline Outreach

Reusable body for first-contact wholesale pricing emails. Placeholders in
`{braces}`. Keep the sent version under ~200 words — it should read like a
florist opening an account, not a survey.

**Subject:** `Wholesale pricing inquiry — AFUVAI (Las Vegas florist)`

---

Hi {vendor team / rep name if known},

I'm Brian, owner of AFUVAI, a floral studio in Las Vegas doing weekly event
and bulk work. I'm setting up my wholesale sourcing and would love to open an
account with you{branch note, e.g. " — I'd be buying out of your {branch}
location"}.

Could you send me current pricing on the stems I buy most? Noting the unit
you quote in (per stem / bunch / box) and grade would help me compare apples
to apples:

- Roses — standard, garden, and spray
- Dahlias, peonies (in season), ranunculus, lisianthus
- Hydrangea, stock
- Eucalyptus (silver dollar / seeded), leather leaf / ruscus

A few account questions while I have you:

- What do you need from me to set up a wholesale account (resale license, application)?
- Minimum order, and delivery days/windows to Las Vegas — or is it will-call?
- Payment terms for new accounts (COD vs net-30)?
- Any weekly standing-order requirement to keep pricing status?
- Pre-order cutoffs for Valentine's Day / Mother's Day?
- Who would my sales rep be for a new account?

Thanks so much — looking forward to working with you.

Brian
AFUVAI Society, Las Vegas

---

## Usage notes

- One email per vendor **company**, not per branch. Where a company has
  multiple branches (FSS, Greenleaf), name the branch you'd buy from in the
  branch note and ask them to route you.
- To = verified sales email from `../vendor-contacts.json` only. If email is
  null there, do not invent one — use `../phone-script.md` instead.
- Drafts only. Never auto-send; Brian reviews and sends from his Gmail.
